## Key Decisions

- **Tier floors, now measured:** `textTertiary` is the 3:1 floor (10–13px hints, timestamps,
  column headers; ~250 sites). `textMuted` stays UNDER 3:1 on purpose — it is the placeholder /
  disabled tier, and lifting it to 3:1 puts it on top of textTertiary and collapses the hierarchy.
  Anything that prints information must use textTertiary or above (#384).
- **APCA scores light-on-dark harshly.** The first solver pass targeted APCA Lc 32 for textMuted
  and produced alpha 0.46 on the dark themes (WCAG 4.7:1, above textTertiary). Retargeted to the
  light themes' measured WCAG level (~1.9:1) instead. Use WCAG as the gate, APCA as the tiebreaker.
- **Status pills are labels, not body text:** 3:1 is the bar for the 9–13px pill text on the light
  themes. The stricter 4.5:1 set was computed (in the mock's "alt" list) and NOT applied — it
  darkens Sunset's greens to `#015a39` territory and Fega didn't ask for it.
- **Dim/Border tints follow their solid.** When a status hex moves, its `rgba(...)` tint bases move
  with it (Sunset green/orange, Paper orange) — same convention as the rest of the file.
