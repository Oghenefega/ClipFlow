Media tab = watched folders Point it at your folders in Settings (like the audio library). New files appear automatically; nothing is copied. The panel itself only speaks in Images / GIFs / Videos — folder names never show. Drop-to-import still exists for one-offs. Upload tab retires.
NLE layering The clip is always the background. Overlay 2 draws on top of Overlay 1. "+" adds a track (up to 3). Blocks drag/trim/Alt-duplicate exactly like sound blocks, and stick to their moment through cuts.
On-canvas control Click an overlay in the preview to get the frame + corner handles — drag to move, corners to resize. Size/position are % of the output frame, so render matches preview exactly.
Video sound on by default Reaction clips play their own audio mixed into the clip (🔊 60% on the block); each video overlay gets a volume slider + mute. Extra SFX/Music tracks come in the last batch — the "+" on SFX shows where.
