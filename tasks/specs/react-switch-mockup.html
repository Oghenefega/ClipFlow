6Not in the first version

Corva guessing "watching" vs "playing" on its own. In the first version you flip the switch yourself. Teaching the game-guess to tell a trailer from real play comes later, once it's been tested for accuracy.
Reacting to a game you don't have in your library. There's no game to put a switch on, so it stays a content type. Adding one straight from the Rename tab (#337) covers that.
Reactions teaching each other. A new reaction entry starts with no learning history. Sharing "what makes a good reaction clip" across games is a later idea.
